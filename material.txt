The “buffer” is in the switch, not in Ryu.

A packet misses the flow table. The switch may store (buffer) the full packet in its own memory and send the controller only a PacketIn containing a buffer_id plus (often) just the headers.
Controller replies (PacketOut or FlowMod) referencing that buffer_id so the switch can forward the already‑stored packet without the controller resending the payload.
If the switch did NOT buffer the packet, it sets buffer_id = OFP_NO_BUFFER and includes the entire packet bytes in msg.data. Then the controller MUST put the bytes back into the PacketOut (data=msg.data) because the switch has no copy.
